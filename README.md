# Immigration guidance: South Korea

Published guidance that the authorities of South Korea issue to the officials who decide immigration cases, collected in November 2026.

- **Guidance:** 211 files in this folder. `index.tsv` lists each file with its title, date and source address.
- The files are unmodified copies of what the source published. Where a source refused automated access, copies come from a public web archive; the index says which.
- What is missing, and how to obtain it, is described in `NOTES.txt` and `not-obtained.tsv` here, and for all jurisdictions in [MISSING.md](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines/.github/blob/main/MISSING.md), in the repository that also holds the download scripts. Other jurisdictions are listed on the [organisation page](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines).

The core of this repository is the Korea Immigration Service's two manuals, published on Hi Korea and reissued in place every few weeks: the visa manual by status (사증민원 자격별 안내 매뉴얼) and the sojourn manual by status (체류민원 자격별 안내 매뉴얼), both in the November 2026 edition, with their change log. They are in HWP format, the Korean word-processor format in which they are published. `hikorea/` also holds scheme manuals and guideline notices; `law.go.kr/` holds the Ministry of Justice's directives, established rules and notices on immigration, nationality and refugees that are in force. Earlier editions of the manuals are not kept by the source, and the ministry's full internal guidelines are not published.

The documents remain the work of the authorities that published them.
